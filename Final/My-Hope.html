My Hope & Mission

My hope in everything is that I might not be ashamed of the Gospel, so that others might find the great treasure, and that I honor Christ in my life and through my death. (Philipians 1:20) I have so many other "side quests" but really it's just the reality that comes about by implementing "Christ be honored in life and death" in me. Here's a couple examples: loving God with all my heart, soul, mind, and strength; and loving everyone (aka my neighbor) as myself (Matthew 22:36-40), taking the Gospel to all tribes and nations (Mark 16:15-16), and living in a manner worthy to the Gospel (Ephesians 4:1). All of these overflow from striving to honor God in everything.
