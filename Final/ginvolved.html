How to Get Involved

There are multiple ways to get involved. If you're a relatively new Christian that hasn't found a good, Bible-believing group of local believers (church), I wouldn't recommend starting a new ministry. Instead, I would be quick to join a local church, ask about opportunities there, and seek out more in the community of churches around you if there aren't enough at your church. I've learned that there's fountains of knowledge to be learned from people who have been faithfully serving for years.

Service Opportunities to Look For

If you're in an area where homelessness is an issue, homeless outreachs can be a great place to start. They combine serving the poor with giving financially, which is more blessed (Acts 20:35). You can also start with searching out elderly or disabled citizens that could use some help with various tasks. It doesn't matter what you do (within reason) as much that you just do something significant to serve. There's essential benefits that flow from it into our own and others spiritual health, not to mention the new ideas, interactions, and missions it opens you up to.
