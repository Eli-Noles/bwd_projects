What does it mean for Christ to be our anchor?

The anchor metaphor isn't found in the Bible describing Christ (although it can be used for Christ), but it is used to describe the opposite of faith in Him. James 1:6 says that doubt leads a person to be "like a surging sea, driven and tossed by the wind." Faith in Jesus is the anchor that prevents us from being shaken by the storms, and faith in Jesus means trusting in who He claims to be, and what He promises to do.
